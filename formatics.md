# Formatics: Defining a Research Paradigm

## *A meta-theoretical framework on "what Formatics is, what it studies, and how to study it"*

---

This paper is not a case study. It is a toolbox.

The preceding papers — on dog-whistle methodology, full-stomach feudalism, and instant god-making — each addressed an empirical phenomenon. But they share a common underlying logic. The task of this paper is to extract that logic, name it, define it, delineate its boundaries, and provide an operational manual.

Its function is to ensure that later researchers — whether studying China, India, Japan, or the Middle East — know what the word "Formatics" means. And whether its framework can be used to analyze their own cases.

---

## 1. Definition

Formatics studies how a premodern power-ethics complex, under modern material conditions, switches from a low-power survival mode to a full-power operational mode, and in the process generates increments. New rules, new classes, new financial conduits, new forms of legitimation — these are all increments. The object of study is the encoding mechanisms through which this complex formats human behavior: how humans are shaped into units that automatically execute system commands without external coercion.

The term "formatting" is borrowed from computing: once a disk is formatted, the file system written onto it determines how data is stored, how it is read, and who can write. Formatting a person means that a set of rules is written into the cognitive substrate. Not at the level of "I know this is how this should be done," but at the level of "this is just how this thing is done, period." Bride price is not "I choose to pay it" — it's "it just has to be paid." Continuing the lineage is not "I think we should have a son" — it's "if I don't have a son, my entire life has been wasted."

The core research questions of Formatics:

1. How is formatting accomplished? What mechanisms write rules into the human cognitive substrate? What material conditions do these mechanisms require?
2. How does formatting switch modes under different material conditions? Why does it downclock during famine? Why does it go to full throttle after subsistence is secured? What does the same rule set output at different economic levels?
3. Why can the formatting system produce increments? Not just repeatedly executing old rules — why, once materially sufficient, does it start making new rules, new classes, new gods, new financial instruments?
4. What is the exit cost of the formatting system? Why do people who are visibly being consumed by the system remain inside, running ever faster? What does it mean to have no backup script?

## 2. Core Concepts

The following concepts constitute the basic vocabulary of Formatics. They are analytical tools, not descriptive labels.

### 2.1 Feudal OS

A power-ethics complex that a civilization developed during its agrarian era, with bloodline-based differential order as its routing protocol, ritual / positional naming / taboo naming / orthodoxy as its encoding mechanisms, and self-execution as its enforcement method.

Key characteristics:

- Not sustained by violence. Coercion is secondary and supplementary. The primary mechanism is the self-management of the formatted.
- Not top-down design. There is no "chief architect." The system is an emergent collective order produced by countless people each executing local rules. No one ever sat in a room and drew up this blueprint.
- Cross-regional variants share a single underlying architecture. China's clan system, India's caste system, Japan's *ie* household system, European feudal hierarchy. The differences lie in the specific rules; the commonality lies in the formatting logic.

### 2.2 Formatting

The process of writing social norms into an individual's cognitive substrate so that they operate as default protocols. A fully formatted person:

- No longer recognizes "this is an artificial set of rules." The rules become "naturally this way."
- Executes rules without calculating costs and benefits. "It just has to be this way."
- Spontaneously rejects those who violate the rules. No command from above is needed.

The completion marker of formatting is self-command. Holding a gun to your head and telling you to pay bride price — that is coercion. You yourself feeling that *not* paying bride price is wrong — that is formatting. The formatted person is not locked in. He holds the key himself, locks the door himself, and then swallows the key.

### 2.2a The Underlying Protocol: name → position → ritual → action → reality

Formatting does not execute in scattered points. It relies on a five-character drive shaft, running from the Zhou *li* through to the present, that converts "an assigned name" into "a lifetime of automatically executed behaviors" and ultimately produces "a person defined by formatting."

**Name.** First, naming. The system gives you a name — Zhao family descendant, first son of the eldest son, academic household — not one you chose. Name is the entry point of formatting. Confucius' "if names are not correct, speech will not be in accordance" is the first theoretical statement to identify the power of naming as the system's entry gate.

Name has two entry points, not just birth:

- Birth-assigned name (default path): assigned at birth. Eldest grandson, so-and-so's daughter — factory settings, not options.
- Mid-course renaming (at any time): the system has the capacity to reopen the naming gate at any point beyond factory settings. A woman, upon marrying into her husband's family, is renamed "so-and-so's wife." A nouveau riche who funds the rebuilding of an ancestral hall gets a new positional name. Zhao Shizi is given a name after just two years of design — no need for centuries of accumulation. Name is not only available at birth — the system is more flexible than you think, and opens the gate whenever needed.

**Position.** Once the name takes hold, position is determined.[^1] Which ring of the ripple you occupy, who are your elders and juniors, toward whom you bear obligations — position is the spatialization of name. The differential order is essentially a topological map of position. Fei Xiaotong described the outward-propagation structure of ripples, but the ripples themselves are merely the carrier of position — the positional-name coordinates come first, and the direction of the ripples follows.

**Ritual.** Once position is clear, behavioral norms emerge. Filial piety, fraternal deference, loyalty, trustworthiness, what should be refused, when silence should speak — ritual is position's automated execution manual. People are not "choosing to follow ritual" — people, having soaked in their assigned position long enough, find that their bodies automatically do the right thing.

**Action.** Once ritual is internalized, behavior executes automatically. No supervision needed, no punishment needed, no one needs to remind. The fully formatted person will kowtow in the ancestral hall of their own accord, will send red envelopes in the family group chat of their own accord, will feel, when county-town housing prices rise, that they should buy a house of their own accord. The system does not need to assign someone to watch you — you watch yourself.

**Reality.** The final product. After name → position → ritual → action complete a full cycle, the output is "reality" — a person defined by formatting. Your name is in the genealogy, your reputation stands in the village, you are on the @ list in the family group chat, you are a line item in your father's face ledger. Reality is not objective fact — it is fact defined by formatting. A person "succeeded" or "went astray" — this does not refer to what he did, but to the fact that the reality he produced does not match the name he was assigned at birth. Note one thing: the reality field records the outcome, not the process. How you got there — reality does not record. The system only cares whether the final product meets spec. What means were used along the way, how many unnamed exits were passed through — none of this appears on the KPI dashboard.

These five links idled slowly in the low-power era (rules existed, no one had time to run them) and run in full-power linkage under performance mode (name → position → ritual → action → reality executing at full speed, gears fully meshed). The five-character protocol is the drive shaft of the formatting system — the phenomena addressed in the preceding papers (dog whistles, bride price, god-making) are each partial manifestations of localized forces or fractures at different points along this drive shaft.

This drive shaft does not rely on violence during normal operation.[^2] No one holds a gun to your head to make you kowtow in the ancestral hall. But this is a runtime characteristic, not the whole system — historically, ritual has always had a violence backup: illicit cults smashed by government troops, clan vigilante justice, widows who failed to maintain chastity drowned in ponds. The system does not use violence, on the premise that you are already running inside the system. The moment you attempt to exit, the immune response activates. Non-violence is a runtime characteristic; violence is an immune-state characteristic — the two coexist, each managing its own domain.

[^1]: Naming is necessary, not sufficient. Not all naming successfully converts to positional placement. "Eldest grandson" is a name — but if this person left for the city at 18, hasn't returned to the village in 40 years, and has no position in the ancestral hall, his position is empty. Naming occurred; placement did not. There is a reception-rate problem between name and position — it's not 100%.

[^2]: The violence boundary was pointed out by Reviewer A, to whom thanks are due. The original protocol's statement that "it does not rely on violence to operate" holds under normal conditions but failed to note the role violence plays during exit.

### 2.3 Low-Power Mode / Performance Mode

The two operating states of the formatting system under different material conditions:

| | Low-Power Mode | Performance Mode |
|---|----------|----------|
| Trigger condition | Scarce subsistence resources | Abundant subsistence resources |
| Rule execution | Simplified, deferred, discounted | Full-quantity, real-time, no discounts |
| Face competition | Floor effect (small gaps) | No ceiling (gaps can widen indefinitely) |
| Exit possibility | Physically impossible to exit | Physically possible to exit, not necessarily cognitively |
| Incremental production | None — maintenance only | Present — new rules, new classes, new tools |
| System overhead | Vast majority of energy spent on survival | Surplus energy flows entirely into formatting pipes |

Low-power mode is not "no feudal ideas." It's that the rules are all there but the CPU had no idle cycles. Performance mode is not "feudalism came back." It's that the rules were always there, and the CPU finally has cycles to run them.

The corollary of this model: modernization is not feudalism's enemy — it is the sustenance feudalism has been waiting three thousand years for. It's not "the material fruits of modernization should have dissolved feudalism but failed to." It's "feudalism has always been waiting for sufficient material conditions to run at full power." Like an ancient engine that has finally found fuel suited to its design.

### 2.4 Incremental Production

When the formatting system enters performance mode, it not only accelerates the execution of old rules, but also produces new things not found in the old rule set. This includes:

- New gods: not spontaneously emerging, slowly accumulated folk legends of efficacy — but sacred products drawn by a designer, legitimated by dream revelation, launched through live-streaming.
- New classes: not traditional religious functionaries (shrine keepers, Daoist priests, monks) — but the private owners and agents of gods (the *dongjia*).
- New financial conduits: not intentionally designed money-laundering networks — but unregulated capital channels that automatically emerge from the combination of cross-border clan networks, anonymous cash donation traditions, and ICH tax-exempt status.
- New forms of legitimation: not illicit cults suppressed by the state — but gods certified by official ICH designation, entering state-owned blind boxes, and incorporated into local cultural tourism GDP.

Incremental production is what distinguishes Formatics from the traditional sociological narrative of "feudal remnants." "Remnants" imply stock in decline — just hasn't disappeared yet. Increments mean the system is manufacturing new things. Not lingering residue. Growth.

### 2.5 The Dog-Whistle Mechanism (Four Morphisms)

Dog whistle: a sender transmits a superficially harmless signal, and receivers — and only formatted receivers — can decode a command within it. This mechanism has four mutually irreducible forms:

1. Authorless / Ritual: the command has no traceable source. "Who decided bride price should be ¥200,000?" No one. Yet simultaneously everyone knows "that's the going rate." Social compulsion without a subject.

2. Hollow / Positional Naming: the signal's content is empty. "Zhao Shizi is a high-aesthetics deity" — but the receiver fills it in using their own formatted cognition. A word, a title, an identity tag — no need to carry information; only need to trigger the encoding already present in the receiver.

3. Negative-Space / Taboo Naming: the signal's power lies in what it does not say. *Ligong* replaces *nongmingong.* The missing stroke (the protruding stroke of 力 absent, leaving 刀) carries more information than what is written. The tabooed thing itself becomes the loudest presence.

4. Recursive / Orthodoxy: the output of the last round of dog whistling becomes the input for the next. Taboo produces new words, new words produce new taboos. The loop converges infinitely toward "orthodoxy" — an endpoint beyond questioning.

Dog whistles are not conspiracy. No one "designed" these signals. They are the signal-transmission protocol of the formatting system running naturally.

## 3. Object of Study and Boundaries

### 3.1 What Formatics Studies

- Behavioral norms in any society that have been formatted to the "default protocol" level — where the executor is no longer aware they are executing a rule
- The encoding, transmission, and execution mechanisms of these norms
- How changes in material conditions alter the operating mode of the formatting system
- The switching conditions under which the formatting system moves from "maintaining itself" to "incremental production"

### 3.2 What Formatics Does Not Study

- Norms with explicit written legal provisions enforced by state violence (such as criminal law, tax law). Formatics is concerned with things where "no one is holding a gun to your head, but you do it anyway."
- Pure personal preference or psychological phenomena. Not all unconscious behavior is formatting — "I'm used to drinking coffee" and "I'm used to feeling my life is wasted without a son" are not the same thing.
- Intentionally designed political propaganda or commercial advertising. These have clear authors, traceable sources, measurable effects. Dog whistles are authorless; propaganda has an author. This is a fundamental distinction.

### 3.3 Boundary Case: When "Authored" Becomes "Authorless"

The most important boundary case occurs at the moment when an originally intentional rule gradually loses its designer and is naturalized by society into "the way it just is."

A magistrate, years ago, ordered: "No one in this village is to mention that matter." Two generations later, no one remembers the order. But no one mentions that matter. This is not propaganda — the source of propaganda has already vanished. What remains is formatting.

The expertise of Formatics lies precisely here: identifying traces of formatting's operations at the blurred boundary between "power" and "default order."

## 4. Methodology

### 4.1 Conceptual Analysis

Formatics relies on ideal-type construction. Extract core structures from empirical phenomena, temporarily strip away empirical noise, and construct a logically coherent pure conceptual model. This is not empirical research — its validation lies not in statistical significance but in the precision of structural mapping and the typicality of cases.

The risk of conceptual analysis is detachment from experience. The defense: every concept must have at least two empirical anchors. Mention "formatting" — must simultaneously provide instances of formatted behavior and non-formatted control behavior. Mention "incremental production" — must place a concrete incremental product on the page. Zhao Shizi's design sketch, a specific village's You Shen donation list — concepts without empirical anchors are not permitted in the main text.

### 4.2 Historical Comparison

Analyzing the formatting system's mode switch (low-power → performance) requires a diachronic control. The key method: systematic comparison of the execution mode, intensity, and cost of the same behavior in the hunger era and the full-stomach era.

- Same place, different time: long-term panel data on bride price amounts in the same region
- Same behavior, different execution intensity: comparing "lineage continuation" in female-infanticide-prevalent areas versus contemporary second-son pursuit
- Different civilizations, same structure: parallel comparison of Chinese bride-price inflation and Indian dowry inflation

The trap of comparison: do not use one civilization's ideal type to validate another civilization's ideal type. The correct procedure is to start from empirical phenomena, then identify the formatting structure behind them. The order cannot be reversed.

### 4.3 Fieldwork

Conceptual analysis and historical comparison provide the framework; fieldwork fills in the flesh. Formatics requires multiple types of empirical materials:

- In-depth interviews: not "do you believe," but "do you think it's acceptable not to pay bride price" — to test formatting depth
- Participant observation: how much of the You Shen event's financial flows can be observed? What does the public posting of donation lists indicate?
- Archival research: expressions about "sending money home to repair the temple" in overseas Chinese letters. Property transfer records in genealogies.
- Digital traces: the *dongjia's* live-stream data, fans' comment content, donation-amount time series.

The output of fieldwork is not "proof" of the framework. It is pinning the framework to empirical materials, so that subsequent researchers can locate the same empirical soil.

### 4.4 Peer Validation Process

1. Framework publication: core concepts, research boundaries, and methodology presented in full on public platforms.
2. Case alignment: other researchers use the same framework to analyze different cases (different regions, different civilizations).
3. Boundary stress testing: the framework encounters uninterpretable cases in the course of application — framework amended or boundary tightened.
4. Concept convergence: after multiple rounds of amendment, the meanings of core concepts stabilize within the academic community.

## 5. Relationship to Existing Disciplines

Formatics does not replace any existing discipline. It sits at several boundaries:

| Existing field | What Formatics takes from it | What Formatics does not take |
|---------|------------|------------|
| Sociology (Fei Xiaotong tradition) | Differential order, rule by ritual | Functionalist assumptions (system maintains equilibrium) |
| Anthropology (Bourdieu) | Habitus, symbolic capital | Emphasis on "strategic" — formatted behavior is not strategy, it is default |
| Communication studies | Encoding/decoding, media effects | The "sender → receiver" model — dog whistles have no sender |
| Economic anthropology (Goody) | Historical comparison of marriage exchange | Pure economic rationality framework |
| Religious history | Texts on illicit cults, god-making, folk religion | Doctrinal analysis and authenticity judgments of religious experience |

The core contribution of Formatics: bringing the question of "how power makes people manage themselves" from the periphery to the center of these disciplines, and establishing an independent analytical framework for it.

## 6. Cross-Civilizational Comparison Coordinates

What follows is not a cross-civilizational analysis. It is comparison coordinates left for later researchers — pointing out where functional equivalents may exist in other civilizations, without doing the analysis for them.

### 6.1 Cross-Civilizational Variants of Formatting

| Civilization | Core Formatting Mechanism | Possible Corresponding Concepts |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| India | Caste × karma-reincarnation | Dharma as default behavioral encoding; dowry inflation paralleling bride-price inflation |
| Japan | *Ie* household system × *giri-ninjo* | *Giri* as formatting language; *seken-tei* (social face) as society-wide surveillance |
| Middle East | Tribal lineage × honor ethics | Blood revenge as self-execution; *mahr* (dower) formatting vs. non-formatting distinction |
| Latin America | Hacienda system × Catholic hierarchy | Patron-client as personalized formatting; salvation economy paralleling face economy |
| Sub-Saharan Africa | Ancestor worship × elder politics | *Lobola* (bride wealth) commercialization and urbanization effects |

### 6.2 Comparative Methodology

- First, excavate one civilization deeply: use the complete conceptual toolchain (Feudal OS → low-power/performance → incremental production → dog whistles) to analyze one empirical domain in one civilization, producing testable propositions.
- Then, transplant in parallel: bring these propositions as hypotheses into another civilization — not to "prove the framework's universality," but to test at what level the propositions break down. The breakdown point is the comparative discovery.
- No "essentializing": Formatics does not claim "China has been formatted since antiquity" — it claims "under specific historical material conditions, the formatting mechanism was activated to a certain degree." Comparative subjects are handled with the same dynamic framework.

## 7. Research Agenda

What follows is not work already completed in this paper. It is the blank spaces to be filled by later researchers.

### 7.1 Empirical Verification

- Bride-price inflation panel data: multi-decade, multi-province, village-level bride-price tracking, with control variables (sex ratio, per capita income, out-migration rate), testing the explanatory power of the "system command" hypothesis versus the "pure supply-demand" hypothesis.
- *Ligong* population systematic sampling: questionnaire survey + in-depth interviews, measuring formatting depth (bride-price payment willingness, face-competition intensity, lineage-continuation obsession), correlated with material-condition indicators.
- You Shen donation data analysis: statistical analysis of publicly posted donation lists (amount distribution, donor geographic distribution, donor occupation/age), tracing the inflow paths of overseas Chinese funds.

### 7.2 Historical Depth

- Systematic tracking of bride-price amounts since the Qing Dynasty: using local gazetteers, genealogies, marriage contracts, and folk deeds to reconstruct a long time series spanning the low-power period and the performance-switching period.
- The game history of illicit cults and orthodoxy: official documents suppressing illicit cults across dynasties vs. the actual persistence of folk worship — has this offensive-defensive pattern been overthrown in the ICH cultural-creative era?

### 7.3 Cross-Civilizational Comparison

- Chinese bride-price inflation × Indian dowry inflation: a parallel comparison of marriage payments in two agrarian civilizations undergoing modernization — do they share the same formatting logic?
- Overseas Chinese remittance letters × *hawala*: Chinese clan-based You Shen donation cross-border capital flows and the Arab-South Asian *hawala* informal remittance system — are they functionally equivalent?

### 7.4 Contemporary Extensions

- Algorithmic formatting: is the preference function of short-video recommendation algorithms becoming a new encoder for formatting? Does the algorithm's "authorless recommendation" (no one knows why it was pushed, but it pushes) constitute a new form of dog whistle?
- The financialization of bride-price loans: banks turning "completing a system command" into a credit product — is finance serving feudalism, or is finance converting feudalism into a securitizable asset?

## 8. Limitations

This paper (and Formatics as a research paradigm) acknowledges the following fundamental limitations:

1. "Formatting" cannot be directly observed. It is a cognitive state inferred from behavior. You can see a person paying bride price; you cannot see "he feels it just has to be paid." Formatting is a concept with a high operationalization ceiling. Not impossible to measure, but far harder to measure than "income" or "years of education."
2. The cost of ideal-type abstraction. "Feudal OS" is a theoretical construct. Actual rural society is a superposition of clan tradition, socialist administration, and market-consumerist logic. Isolating the formatting component from this superposition is itself a simplification. Simplicity enables analytical clarity, but clarity is not completeness.
3. Proposer bias. This paper is written by the proposer of the concept of Formatics. When defining one's own research paradigm, a natural self-confirmation tendency exists. Correcting this bias can only come from outside: later researchers using this framework to analyze cases, discovering where it breaks down. Those are the places where the limitations of this paper become manifest.
