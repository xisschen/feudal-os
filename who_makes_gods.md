# Who Makes Gods? — Zhao Shizi and the Feudal System's Incremental Production Capacity

## *A personal observation on "what the feudal system gained after being fed was not an accelerator, but a production line"*

---

Ask someone from Changle, Fujian, "Who is Zhao Shizi?" before 2022 — they'll probably stare blankly.

Ask after Spring Festival 2024 — the answer might be "Shut-in, loves fashion, won't go out unless his clothes look right." Like a stan describing their idol's persona.

Zhao Shizi — this "god" — debuted in 2023. Design cycle: roughly two years. Designer: a Gen-Z local named Lin Bin. Design inspiration: a character from the Chinese xianxia animation *Forensic*. The deity's face is an "anime-style sculpt." Because it was literally drawn to look like one.

He has no scripture. No theological genealogy. No body of doctrine. No accumulated good works. No KPI of merit like Mazu's "rescuing people at sea." No moral narrative like Guan Gong's "loyalty and righteousness."

The paternity question is a mess: some say he's the son of Zhao Gongming of the Five Blessings Emperor, but the original Five Blessings legend contains no mention of a "Shizi" (princeling) at all. Taiwan's religious databases return nothing. Local elders can't recall any Zhao Shizi in the You Shen procession. From the standpoint of academic source criticism — he doesn't exist.

And yet: he has been manufactured, worshipped, operated, consumed, and written into an intangible cultural heritage blind-box product.

Zhao Shizi is not a one-off. He belongs to a product matrix called the "Princeling Ensemble" — Zhang Dashizi (loves smoking fancy cigarettes, spoils his little brother), Zhang Ershizi (energetic, always wants to go out), Huaguang Dashizi (three-eyed, initially designed as an 8-year-old who loves QQ candy, later "aged up" into a handsome idol to attract female fans). Each has an independent persona, independent fan base, independent merchandise line.

I want to understand one thing: how exactly a god that doesn't exist got manufactured. This matters not because it happened in Fujian. It matters because it exposes the feudal operating system's capacity for **incremental production** under material abundance. My last paper wrote about "eating well shifted feudalism from low-power to performance mode" — that was about existing stocks: old mechanisms like bride price, face competition, and lineage continuation getting overclocked. But stock acceleration isn't the ceiling. The ceiling is the system generating **new things.** Zhao Shizi is the exhibit from this production line.

### A necessary distinction: This is not "Douyin manufactures internet celebrities"

Douyin churns out hundreds of "gods" and "goddesses" every day. Algorithmic push, live-stream tipping, MCN contracts, irrelevant in three months, replaced by the next batch. That assembly line produces traffic nodes: replaceable, forgettable, without sanctity, never entering a temple.

Zhao Shizi's assembly line produces **gods**: granted authorization via dream revelation, housed in a temple, stamped with intangible cultural heritage certification, privately owned by a *dongjia*. He was not selected by an algorithm. He was drawn by a designer, legitimated by dream revelation, and "activated" by the You Shen ritual.

The two assembly lines look similar. Both involve live-streaming, tipping, fans. But the products enter different warehouses. Douyin internet celebrities enter MCN contracts; Zhao Shizi enters a temple. The former are forgotten in three to five years. The latter — once he joins the You Shen procession, gets ICH certified, and is turned into a blind box — transforms from "forgettable traffic" into "officially documented cultural heritage."

This paper concerns the latter assembly line.

## 1. The Five-Step God-Making Method

The five-step method is not a business process — it is the five-character drive shaft of Formatics (name → position → ritual → action → reality) instantiated at full speed in incremental production mode. Each step corresponds to one gear in the transmission:

- Sketch = **Name.** The system creates a name first. Not "named" after centuries of accumulated miracles — the name exists from the moment the designer draws it.
- Dream revelation = **Position.** A name needs spatial placement. The dream revelation does not confer sanctity — it assigns the name a slot in the divine genealogy. "Zhao Shizi is the son of Zhao Gongming" — true or false, once position is established, the subsequent gears activate.
- Launch & operations = **Ritual.** Once position is fixed, behavioral norms emerge. How to worship, what clothes to wear, whether he can go out, how to interact with fans — these are all ritual. Not "invented" by the *dongjia* — generated collectively by fans swarming around the assigned position.
- Fandom operations = **Action.** Once ritual is internalized, fans execute autonomously. No commands needed. "Voting" is the new donation; "fan support" is the new ritual procession. You do it yourself.
- Legitimation = **Reality.** The final output. ICH certificate, state-owned enterprise blind box, contribution to cultural tourism GDP. A god defined by formatting. Not a question of "do you believe" — it's in a museum and a blind box. That is reality.

The five steps are not a conspiracy. They are a drive train. No one designed the entire chain — but every participant, at their own station, completed the segment assigned to them.

### Step 1: Sketch

Traditional folk god-making normally took centuries. Something inexplicable happened somewhere — a man struck by lightning whose body didn't rot, a fisherman who returned safely from a storm — people began telling stories, slowly accumulating an oral tradition of *lingyan* (efficacy). Someone donated funds for a small shrine. Once incense accumulated to a threshold, Daoist or Buddhist clerics absorbed the figure into the orthodox pantheon, or the authorities denounced it as an "illicit cult" and shut it down. The process was spontaneous, slow, and had no designer.

Zhao Shizi took a different path.

He had a designer. Lin Bin, a local of Changle, member of the Lin lineage of Kefeng Village. He spent roughly two years on Zhao Shizi's facial design — following anime aesthetic standards, not any religious iconographic tradition. The result is a "CG model face" — repeated described in Douyin and Xiaohongshu comment sections as "domineering CEO face," "so hot," "want to marry him."

No one knelt in a temple for centuries begging for miracles before deciding what the god should look like. The god's appearance was a **product design decision.**

### Step 2: Dream Revelation

The sketch is ready. Now it needs a theological license.

"The deity appeared in a dream and instructed that a new statue be made." This sentence itself is nothing new — dream revelation, spirit writing, divine descent: these are the oldest oracle mechanisms in Chinese folk religion. Guan Gong appearing in a vision, Mazu appearing in a dream, the *lingyan* legends of countless local gods — the script has been roughly the same for centuries: a miracle occurs (someone survives against the odds) → oral transmission for decades → a dream confirms "it was I who protected you" → a temple is built and a statue carved. Dream revelation was always the **final step** in the authorization process, arriving only after folk narrative had fermented sufficiently. It was the **closing act**, not the **opening act.**

Zhao Shizi's difference lies not in "using dream revelation." It lies in dream revelation being compressed into a two-year design cycle, and being moved from closing act to opening act.

Traditional god-making: miracle → decades of oral transmission → dream revelation confirmation → temple and statue. Zhao Shizi: sketch → dream revelation → live-stream launch. All within the same year. Previously, "miracle first, dream revelation second" — the event preceded the name. Zhao Shizi reverses this — sketch first, dream revelation second. Name before reality. The statue hadn't been carved yet, but the persona was already set, the live-stream channel was already open, and fans were already asking "is he coming out this year?"

"Dream revelation" here does not function to "confirm a pre-existing miracle." It provides theological legitimacy for a product that is not yet complete. The tool is old. What's new is the speed and direction — previously, miracles pulled the dream revelation; now, the dream revelation pulls the production line.

### Step 3: Launch & Operations

Spring Festival 2023: Zhao Shizi joins the Changle You Shen procession for the first time.

You Shen (god-walking) itself is a roughly four-hundred-year-old tradition — carrying temple statues out of the temple to tour the territory, expelling evil and praying for blessings. But what Zhao Shizi joined was not traditional You Shen. What he joined was a content product already re-encoded by short-video platforms.

The entire procession is live-streamed. Real-time online viewership can reach over a hundred thousand. The stream host guides viewers to send virtual gifts. Deity statues have fixed camera positions. Fans flood the comment stream with "The Shizi looks so good today," "What's the Shizi wearing today?"

### Step 4: Fandom Operations

Zhao Shizi's operations team is called the *dongjia* — a term that is itself revealing. Not "shrine keeper." Not "temple committee." Not "congregation representative." *Dongjia* — meaning "owner" or "proprietor."

Zhao Shizi's *dongjia*, known online as "Lin Laoliu," is based in the United States. His sister, in China, handles the statue's daily upkeep. The *dongjia* decides whether the statue "goes out or stays in," whether it "accepts invitations from other villages." In the live-stream, the *dongjia* says: "My mood is Zhao Shizi's mood."

This is not metaphor. This is a statement of ownership.

The *dongjia* has built a complete persona for Zhao Shizi: "shut-in personality, hard to convince to leave the house, obsessed with appearance, always dressed in black-and-gold dragon robes." Fans project emotion onto this — not "faith," but *fandom.* "Brother looks great today." "The *dongjia* is treating him badly — the styling is outdated, the headpiece fell off." "The top-spending female fan paid to touch the Shizi's face."

In the cognitive architecture of a fandom, Zhao Shizi is not a sacred being to be knelt before. He is a commodity with personality flaws, in need of emotional protection, onto whom feelings can be projected. Worshipping gods and chasing idols use the same behavioral repertoire — chart-topping (donation), fan support (ritual procession), merchandise (blind-box folding fans), fansite hosting (live-stream camera positions).

### Step 5: Legitimation

At the end of 2024, the Changle District Industrial Investment Development Group — a state-owned enterprise — partnered with a cultural creative company to release the *You Shen Tian Tuan* (God-Walking Ensemble) IP blind box. It includes deities like Qiye, Baye, Shengjin Shaoniang, Zhao Shizi, and a rare hidden edition of Huaguang Dadi. Sold at popular tourist spots in Fuzhou such as Sanfang Qixiang and Yantaishan.

This move is the certification stage of the entire god-making assembly line. State-owned enterprise production = official stamp of approval. Blind box = Zhao Shizi being reclassified from an object of "worship" into a "cultural consumer product." Once he enters the category of cultural tourism merchandise, all questions about "illicit cults," "manufacturing gods," and "exploiting religion for profit" can be deflected with three words: **"cultural creative innovation."**

ICH + cultural creative products + state-owned enterprise = three protective umbrellas deployed simultaneously. The legitimation loop is closed.

## 2. The *Dongjia*: A New Class of God-Owners

The traditional temple power structure is roughly: god → shrine keeper / Daoist priest / Buddhist monk (religious functionary) → worshippers (donors). The religious functionary mediates between god and people, theoretically serving the god without owning the god.

The *dongjia* creates an entirely new role: god → *dongjia* (owner/agent) → fans (consumers).

The *dongjia* is not a religious functionary. The *dongjia* does not perform religious rituals (or outsources them). The *dongjia's* core work is operations: designing personas, managing live-streams, controlling intellectual property rights, scheduling ritual procession appearances, managing fan relationships, developing merchandise. The *dongjia* is essentially an idol management agency — the god statue is the talent, fans are consumers and unpaid promoters.

This model is far more efficient than traditional religion:

- No seminary needed. No scripture study, no ascetic practice, no certification from any religious body. You are the *dongjia* because you say you are.
- No faith required. Fans do not need to "believe in" Zhao Shizi — they only need to find him handsome, cute, tragic, or worth protecting. Faith is heavy and demands commitment; fandom is lightweight and swappable.
- Diversified income streams. Traditional incense money relies on voluntary donations from worshippers. The *dongjia's* income comes from: live-stream tips, merchandise sales, fees for invited procession appearances (the *dongjia* admits "inviting us out involves a fee"), statue rental, and public donation lists (a single village, single-day event can list up to ¥430,000).
- Offshore controllable. *Dongjia* in the United States. Statue in Fujian. IP in the cloud. Fans worldwide. No country's regulatory system covers this configuration.

## 3. Where the Money Comes From, Where It Goes

(This section was previously considered as a standalone paper. It is condensed here as economic grounding for the present analysis. A full analysis of cross-border capital circulation will be developed elsewhere.)

The financial scale of Fujian You Shen activities can be glimpsed from scattered public reports:

- Houfu Village, Tantou Town, Changle District: two days of events (10th and 26th of the first lunar month, 2024) cost over a million yuan.
- A single You Shen donation list from a village in Tantou Town: highest single donation ¥30,000, lowest ¥2,000, total approximately ¥430,000.
- One *dongjia* team's total annual spending across multiple events reached the eight-figure range (tens of millions).
- A single deity statue's *tagu* frame can cost tens of thousands to over a hundred thousand yuan, with solid gold accessories separate.
- Source of funds: "Those born in the 80s provide the money, those born in the 90s manage, those born in the 00s contribute the labor." The largest donors are the older generation — including a substantial overseas Chinese contingent.

The 80s cohort — where did this generation's wealth accumulation come from? Partly from domestic business, but Changle has a specific feature: over 200,000 people from Changle live in the United States. For the 2024 Spring Festival event, over 200 Houfu residents returned from the U.S. to attend.

The money these people donate to the You Shen — is it in U.S. dollars or RMB? What is the remittance path? Are there receipts? Is tax due? Who audits this money?

If someone earns money in the U.S. and sends it back to China through formal banking channels, they must declare it, face quota limits, and may trigger anti-money laundering review if the amount is large. If they donate that money to a You Shen event in a Fujian village — dropping cash into the "donation" basket — no one will ask a single question.

No one designed this mechanism. The mechanism grew itself. When the cross-border extension of a lineage network (overseas Chinese) is superimposed on an entirely cash-based, anonymous, unaudited donation tradition — the result is a cross-border capital circulation conduit that falls outside the coverage of any modern financial regulatory system.

No one needs to plan "money laundering." The pipe is just there, open.

## 4. Illicit Cult 2.0: Why This Round of God-Making Is Different

Historically, the term *yinsi* (illicit cult) was used to suppress unauthorized folk worship. Confucius said: "To offer sacrifice to something other than what one ought to sacrifice to — this is called illicit worship; illicit worship brings no blessings." Dynasties across Chinese history suppressed illicit cults with roughly the same logic: orthodox religion has scripture, genealogy, state canonization, and moral edification functions. Illicit cults have none of these — only one thing that is supposedly *ling* (efficacious).

But Zhao Shizi's case makes the term "illicit cult" feel somewhat inadequate.

Illicit Cult 1.0 features: spontaneous emergence, oral transmission, slow accumulation, local in scope, suppressed by authorities. Illicit Cult 2.0 (Zhao Shizi model) features: design-driven, launched overnight, nationwide dissemination, fandom-style operation, officially stamped (state-owned enterprise blind box, ICH cultural creative product).

| | Illicit Cult 1.0 | Illicit Cult 2.0 |
|---|---------|----------|
| Birth speed | Decades to centuries | Two years |
| Production mode | Spontaneous emergence | Designer + team + dream revelation |
| Transmission medium | Oral | Short video + live-streaming |
| Believer structure | Local worshippers | Nationwide fans |
| Revenue model | Incense money | Tips + merchandise + blind box + invitation fees |
| Relationship with authorities | Suppressed | Certified (ICH, cultural creative products) |
| Ownership | Collective | Privately owned by *dongjia* |

The most crucial changes are in the last two rows.

Illicit Cult 1.0 was no one's private property. Illicit Cult 2.0's Zhao Shizi has a clearly identifiable owner — *dongjia* Lin Laoliu. The deity statue is not a public religious facility; the deity statue is a private IP. Did the state-owned blind box producer pay IP licensing fees? To whom? How much? From the cultural creative company through the Changle Industrial Investment Group to the *dongjia* and onward to the United States — not a single transaction in this entire capital chain is traceable in sunlight.

A privately held, internationally mobile, tax-exempt intangible asset wrapped in the protective coating of cultural heritage preservation. We are no longer discussing "feudal superstition." We are discussing an entirely new asset class.

## 5. Returning to the Framework: The Feudal System's Incremental Production Capacity

In my previous paper *Full Stomach, Full Feudalism*, I wrote: when humans no longer need to expend all their energy on subsistence, the liberated energy and resources flow into the pipes the clan system has been laying for two thousand years — bride price, face, lineage continuation. That was stock activation. Old mechanisms running faster.

But Zhao Shizi demonstrates a deeper layer: the system is not merely running old code faster. It is writing new code.

The feudal operating system's incremental production capacity under material abundance includes:

1. **Making new gods.** No scripture needed, no miracles needed, no centuries of accumulation needed. Designer + dream revelation + short video is sufficient.
2. **Making a new class.** The *dongjia* — private owner and agent of a god. Cross-border capital moving through the three domains of faith, cultural creative products, and live-streaming.
3. **Making new financial conduits.** You Shen donations + overseas Chinese + anonymous cash + ICH tax exemption = a de facto cross-border capital thoroughfare.
4. **Making new forms of legitimation.** Authorities don't suppress illicit cults — they issue them ICH certificates and sell blind boxes. Legitimacy is not "being allowed to exist"; it is "being included in local cultural tourism GDP."
5. **Making new forms of silence.** When the system runs at full speed, it produces not only displayable outputs — but also byproducts that cannot be displayed. These byproducts do not exist in any public record. Not because they are hidden — because they are defaulted. Incremental production has its below-waterline portion, and the system's KPI dashboard has no field for it.

The most unsettling conclusion: none of this is conspiracy. It is emergence. No one sat in a room and decided "we will exploit the feudal system to build a cross-border capital network." The system, once materially satiated, self-organized these structures. When Lin Bin drew Zhao Shizi, he may genuinely have only wanted to "innovate traditional culture." When the *dongjia* stayed in the United States, he may genuinely only have been "unable to return to China so I manage remotely." When people put their names on the donation list, they may genuinely only have "wanted to contribute to the village."

But what they collectively produced — something none of them individually intended — is a world-class, unregulated, sacredly-cloaked financial-cultural-traffic complex. No one planned this. No one needed to.

The system does not need conspirators. The system only needs enough people, each executing the small segment of script assigned to them. Assembled, it is a complete machine.

## Coda: Gods in Blind Boxes

Writing this, I recall the *You Shen Tian Tuan* blind box released by the Changle Industrial Investment Group.

A consumer spends a few dozen yuan, opens a blind box, and might pull Zhao Shizi, might pull Huaguang Dashizi, might pull the hidden edition Huaguang Dadi. The moment of unboxing shares the same psychological structure as the moment of worship — you are waiting for a presence higher than yourself to descend into your hands. The difference: the worshipper kneels on a prayer mat; the unboxer sits in a live-stream room. The former tosses incense money; the latter sends virtual gifts. The former prays for safety; the latter prays for the hidden edition.

The deity statue has been moved from temple to blind box. But something happened in transit: when the statue was in the temple, it belonged to everyone. Inside the blind box, it belongs to the *dongjia*.

The old gods feared being disbelieved. The new gods fear being unsold.

That is the entire argument of these three papers, in miniature.
